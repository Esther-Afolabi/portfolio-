How I work

The thinking behind every piece.

Good content is a discipline, not an inspiration. The topic changes every time. The process does not.

Step 01

I start with the reader, not the brief

The brief tells me the topic. The reader tells me the angle. Before I write a word, I want to know what they already believe and what would actually shift their thinking. That's where the piece begins — not at the keyword.

Step 02

I research until I have something real to say

Primary sources, competitor analysis, SME interviews where the project allows. I don't start writing until I have a genuine point of view. Thin research produces thin content — and B2B buyers close to a decision can tell the difference immediately.

Step 03

I write for clarity, then edit for precision

My first draft gets the argument down. My edits cut everything that doesn't earn its place. I'm particularly hard on sentences that sound good but say nothing — those are the ones that quietly erode trust in a brand's voice over time.

Step 04

I optimize for search without writing for algorithms

Keyword intent, on-page SEO, internal linking — I use them as a framework, not a formula. The goal is content that ranks because it deserves to. Not content that ranks for three months before readers bounce off it.
